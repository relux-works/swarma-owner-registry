# Proposed Linux original-attestation connector profile

**PROPOSED: no protected connector or archival verifier is implemented or qualified by this profile.** It selects a bounded archival-read contract for the exact source revisions below. Every identity, reader mapping and custodian assignment in this proposal is proposed, not an appointment. No consumer binding is supplied by this profile.

## Source and custody

The proposed custodian is the source maintainer of `relux-works/swarma-owner-registry`. Its proposed responsibilities include Linux session authentication, disclosure admission, the binding-to-subject mapping, storage integration, bounded consumption and validation of external historical/inclusion evidence at read time, exclusion and qualification of the complete Registry operation. WC1 G31 historical-checkpoint verification and the historical witness/issuer authority evidence it requires are absent external qualification dependencies, owned by the `relux-works/swarma-witness` (WC1) source maintainer or provider custodian. Registry must not implement its own historical authority verifier or claim that existing v0 proof is sufficient. The active-generation upstream dependency is `swarma-user-manager/tools/genjournal`, owned by the user-manager source maintainer / format owner; Registry consumes its release-authorizing API and must not reimplement or bypass it. The source maintainer of `relux-works/swarma-keymaker` retains custody of the unchanged prepared-certificate publication dependency. Neither role acquires authority to appoint an issuer, install a provider or override an owner hold.

## Proposed self-disclosure and signed binding

Select a kernel-authenticated Linux UID and its active generation as the self-reader. Retain UID/GID/PID from the accepted connection, current generation, active binding and finite session lifetime. Revalidate them at open and release while holding the generation/authority exclusions. Missing kernel credentials, stale/reused UID, retired generation, revoked binding, expired session or missing continuity refuses. Request-supplied identity, owner/key strings and publication permission establish no reader authority.

Before opening an original body lease, the protected mapping must admit the exact content ID, wire digest, `attestation/1`, owner, subject and independently protected purpose-5 issuer/domain. It must bind that record to the admitted session's binding and active principal. The adapter parses the **exact retained original bytes**, without reserialization, and compares **all six signed `body.binding` fields** to that admitted binding: `uid`, `generation`, `owner`, `path`, `registration_number`, `master_epoch`. Compare integer values as the existing bounded parser's exact integers, not floats or lossy conversions. The core separately verifies original content ID, digest, closed profile and issuer signature; parsing/equality alone does not authenticate the source of the admitted binding.

Repeat the signed binding and protected subject/owner/issuer/ID comparisons before successful final `Lease.Check`, against the same immutable original and session view. Keep `Provenance` unchanged; UID and generation do not become new provenance fields. Retain exact session, mapping, publication, policy and exclusion identities through final check, provenance equality and close. A changed final binding refuses with no returned wire or provenance.

Publishing permission alone grants no read. A publisher that independently satisfies this self-reader policy may read its own admitted original; publishing status is neither sufficient permission nor a blanket denial. Another UID is a separate refusal case. No general owner-admin, cross-owner or cross-subject reader is selected.

This authenticates the endpoint opener only under a qualified per-UID Linux boundary. It cannot distinguish executables sharing a UID or prevent forwarding by an authorized opener. Shared-UID worker isolation is not qualified. The example UID `1002`, generation `generation-2`, owner `owner-a`, path `message/agent-2`, registration number `3`, epoch `epoch-1` and existing public subject/issuer keys are inert fixture values, not appointments. No new account, key or authority mapping is provisioned.

## Publication at intake and proof at archival read time

The archival purpose is exact original retrieval after the original's expiry, without asserting current-use eligibility. This requires two distinct admissions; neither substitutes for the other.

**Intake-time publication admission.** Preserve Registry design §5.5's exact existing bundle, verified while its candidate checkpoint and exact snapshot meet their currentness rules:

1. Exact original signed record/content ID and issuer/authority evidence.
2. Exact unsigned record-add `E_n`, binding sequence, operation, record type and original ID.
3. Exact candidate signed `C_(n+1)` and `E_(n+1)` for the cumulative reissued registry-owner snapshot.
4. Complete oldest-first anchored suffix, including authenticated raw `E_(n-1)` and protected prior `C_(n-1)` under the pre-transition witness's `checkpoint_id`.
5. Exact registry-owner snapshot selected by that checkpoint.
6. Independently verified exact witness intent and committed resolution, both receipts, complete candidate inventory and fresh challenged head/control continuity proving that exact checkpoint resolved.

`C_(n+1).head` is the journal entry ID, never the original record ID. Authenticate scope/root/owner, signatures, pre-transition ancestry, resolution and exact record membership. Retain the exact complete bundle, admission result, original trusted-time premises, watermark and continuity anchors atomically before acknowledgement. Local Store commit, Keymaker's opaque retained reply, a signature/digest or identifiers in `Provenance` cannot establish this admission.

**Read-time obligation — PROPOSED, unavailable in v0.** `archivalView.VerifyArchivalRead` must obtain a fresh challenge-bound authoritative current head/control view in the original read operation; authenticate it under independently protected current authority, qualified time, full required lineage and restore/exclusion floors; and validate externally qualified evidence that the *same* exact historical publication remains an included committed resolution on that current lineage. This means checking the retained `E_n`'s exact sequence/op/type/content ID and wire, historical `C_(n+1)`/snapshot, exact intent/resolution/receipts and candidate inventory against the retained intake admission, then linking them without gaps, forks or replacements to the newly challenged current view. Historical issuer/witness authority at their original verified times must be evidenced by the absent external WC1 G31 qualification dependency, owned by the swarma-witness (WC1) source maintainer or provider custodian, not inferred from present pins or archived signatures. Registry consumes and validates that evidence within the bounded read-time obligation; it does not provide a historical authority verifier of its own.

Freshness attaches to the **new current read head and its applicable current checkpoint/snapshot context**, not to the age of the original certificate. Preserve the design's applicable <=60-second currentness checks for current checkpoint/snapshot evidence and all qualified-clock rules. The old intake checkpoint/snapshot were checked at intake; they are not passed to legacy high-level verification later as if still current. A retained success bit is insufficient: the proposed Registry integration needs externally qualified historical verification and authority evidence, exact retained admission evidence with authenticated custody and uninterrupted independent continuity, plus the fresh inclusion link. Missing intake evidence, read-time inclusion input, current head, historical authority, lineage, floor or exclusion refuses.

This is a proposed **bounded integration of externally qualified historical/inclusion evidence with existing records**, not a new wire format or an implemented algorithm. WC1 G31 historical-checkpoint verification and historical witness/issuer authority evidence remain absent external dependencies owned by the swarma-witness (WC1) source maintainer or provider custodian. Registry only consumes and validates those inputs; it does not qualify them by implementing a local historical authority verifier. The v0 design explicitly says "verified at intake, not historical re-verification"; v0 has no historical verifier. `journal.Verify`, `BuildPublication` and `ReduceProjection` do not supply the missing read-time seam. `ReduceProjection` is a relationship/fork/rollback reducer; external authentication must precede it. Retain all eight authenticated projection fields and original proofs before deriving the existing six-field verifier fact; do not promote a received tuple to authority.

All retained historical inputs and newly supplied current evidence must fit the existing complete-proof/carrier bounds and one operation below. The existing J route admits at most 64 entries including the anchor (at most 63 new entries), two checkpoints and 540672 decoded proof bytes. Protected prior-checkpoint context is charged additionally in the complete carrier. Do not truncate, page, chain expired intermediate admissions, silently switch routes or add retries. A historical-to-current link requiring more evidence than these caps is **unavailable**, even under the proposal. Thus the intended archival scope permits an aged/expired original only where its whole authenticated link can fit; it does not promise arbitrary history depth. No existing 60-second/63-new-entry v0 proof is claimed to qualify an expired archival read. Until the separate proposed seam is implemented and qualified, **protected archival reads remain unavailable**, including the fixture's expired positive original.

Proof verified and retained before the operation is not newly transferred bytes in this operation, but it is not renewed authority. Its exact custody and historical premises must survive, and read-time continuity/inclusion must still be newly checked. Newly transferred challenges, control history, proof and reconciliation traffic belong to the same aggregate/time budget.

At exact Keymaker PR64, `Handoff.PublishedWire()` always returns unavailable. `Retry` preserves original transaction/bytes; `RetainReply` retains a bounded unverified reply. Neither provides publication success or restart custody. That dependency remains closed pending its source-owned publication/reconciliation integration. This proposal does not change or bypass it.

## Continuity, recovery and exclusions

Select the existing protected registry-owner policy and independent witness-side bridge/control authority described in Registry design §§6–7 and Keymaker `spec/witness-service.md`, as proposed roles, not installed authorities. The Registry custodian validates their evidence; it cannot appoint them, acquire witness append authority, bootstrap missing state or override owner restore decisions. The registry writer has no witness append credential.

Before serving and throughout the operation, retain off-node continuity, independently protected latest state/head/checkpoint and restore floor, complete recoverable approved inventory/outcomes and surviving independent old-instance exclusion. On startup/restore, obtain the fresh challenged head and verify lineage/resolution, then reconcile the same expected state using existing `Open(root, expectedStateID)`/`Reconcile(expectedStateID)` against the independent floor. The bridge/control integration and approved-inventory installation API are absent qualification dependencies, not existing methods.

Refuse lower positions, same-position conflicting heads/checkpoints, changed/missing history, unknown boot/continuity, restored clones, unresolved intent, missing floors or lost exclusion. Equal local bytes or a new timestamp cannot clear uncertainty. Only evidence-bound owner-authorized recovery can resume the same lineage; no reset, alternate original, new stream, automatic genesis or second authority store is selected.

Use the design's proposed lock order: publication exclusion → authority/generation exclusion → local Store mutex. Authenticate remote proof before the final local gates, then compare exact identities and retain exclusions through final check and release. Admission is nonblocking, without a queue. No remote proof fetch, signer, RPC or clock-provider call occurs under Store's mutex. The outer exclusions remain absent; Store's own mutex and lifetime flock do not implement them.

## Proposed operation and resource bounds

The selected maximum elapsed local operation is **exactly 1 second**, a proposed resource profile, not measured timing or trusted evidence time. The caller supplies its original finite absolute deadline with positive remaining time <=1 second. Preserve it through open, read, checks and cleanup; no background timeout, method renewal or retry deadline. A connector unable to enforce this inside every blocking method remains unqualified.

| Quantity | Bound |
| --- | --- |
| Operation/concurrency | One active operation per Reader and proposed connector, no queue; one open, no retries, redirects, decompression or alternate provider. |
| Original | 8192 bytes, duplicate-free depth 16; existing core reads at most 8193 including sentinel. Storage must preflight 8192 before allocation/read. |
| Witness wrapper | 8192 bytes including trailing LF, complete original wrapper; no new frame. |
| J proof | 64 entries including anchor, 2 checkpoints, 540672 decoded bytes; each record <=8192/depth 16. All prior context and historical/current links also fit the carrier and time bounds. |
| Complete received bundle | 2097152 bytes; existing `store.Preflight` enforces its shape/count/record/carrier limits but authenticates nothing. |
| Diagnostic body | Proposed 1024-byte prebuffer limit; release only stable existing errors, no original/provenance on refusal. Keymaker's opaque 8192-byte reply limit is separate. |
| Aggregate actual transfer | 2097152 bytes for the whole operation: original, request/control, complete carriers/wrappers, proof transport, diagnostics and cleanup; repeated bytes and encoding/frame overhead count again. An individually valid bundle may exceed this aggregate when combined with the original. |
| Read freshness | Fresh challenge in the same original operation, qualified current evidence and independent continuity/floors; no stale unchallenged view. Historical evidence obeys the separate proposed admission above. |

The external `Attestation(contentID string) ([]byte, error)` port is documented in `internal/grantattestation/README.md` and mentioned in `read.go`; **neither pinned tree implements that callable port**. A future projection returns original wire only. Because that signature carries no context, create one port instance per operation bound to that operation's original finite context; never cache or reuse it to renew deadlines. Provenance stays internal.

The core cannot cancel a blocking connector or meter hidden transfer. Unknown storage, transport, proof, error, aggregate, timing or exclusion bounds refuse. No resource timer supplies trusted evidence time.
